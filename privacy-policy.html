Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

IndiGreen Windows and Doors ("IndiGreen", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry details. When you use our enquiry form, you may give us your name, phone number, email address, city, project details and message.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we receive the details you choose to share, such as photos and sizes of your openings.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting and delivery providers may automatically record basic information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange a site visit and prepare a quote.
  • To manage orders, installation and after-sales support.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.
  • To follow the law and protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

How the enquiry form works

When you press "Send enquiry", your details are sent by a third-party form service (formsubmit.co) to our email inbox at indigreenwinn@gmail.com. When you press "Send on WhatsApp" or contact us by email, your WhatsApp or email app opens with your message, and that message is then handled under the policies of WhatsApp or your email provider.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts to display text, and its pages are delivered by our hosting providers. These providers may process technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. Our links to WhatsApp and email open outside this website.

Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics or similar tools in future, we will update this policy.

Sharing your information

We share information only with people who need it to serve you, such as our manufacturing, delivery and installation teams and service providers who help run this website, and where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you, complete your project and meet legal or accounting needs, and then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you may withdraw your consent to us contacting you. Email us at indigreenwinn@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time, in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
