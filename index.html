uPVC or aluminium?

Both are rust-free and long lasting. Which one fits depends on the room and the view.

uPVC

Best for insulation and everyday value.

  • Excellent thermal and sound insulation
  • Tight seals against dust and rain
  • Low maintenance, no repainting
  • Great for bedrooms and noisy locations

Aluminium

Best for slim lines and large openings.

  • Slimmer frames, bigger glass area
  • Strong enough for wide sliding and folding doors
  • Wide range of powder-coat and wood-look finishes
  • Great for living rooms, balconies and villas

Will uPVC windows leak in heavy rain?

Good uPVC systems have drainage channels and sealing gaskets that let water drain out instead of coming in. Accurate measuring and proper installation matter just as much, which is why our team surveys and fits every job.

Should I choose uPVC or aluminium?

uPVC is a great choice for insulation, sound control and low maintenance, especially in bedrooms and busy areas. Aluminium gives slimmer frames and suits wide sliding or folding openings. We can recommend a mix for different rooms.

Will the windows reduce noise and heat?

Multi-chamber frames with double glazing reduce both. The result depends on the glass you choose, so tell us what bothers you most and we will suggest the right option.

Do the frames rust, rot or attract termites?

uPVC does not rust or rot and termites do not eat it. Powder-coated aluminium resists rust too. Neither needs repainting.

Do you take measurements at my site?

Yes. We visit, measure each opening and give you a written quote. Every window and door is made to the measured size.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the number of openings, sizes and finishes. We share the timeline in your quote after the site survey.

What warranty do you offer?

We share the warranty terms in writing with your quote, so you can read them before you decide.

How do I clean and maintain them?

Wipe frames and glass with mild soap and water. Clear the track of dust now and then and lightly lubricate the hardware once or twice a year.
